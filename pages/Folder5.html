Nine

Fall 2024
Ink on Bristol Board

Nine is a series of nine different abstract geometric drawings. Each drawing is independent from the others. Nine was largely a technical exercise with geometric compositions.
Starting from the top left drawing and going right then down they are titled: Tower, Holiday, Opus 9 Number 1, Gamble, ninety-five, Cycle, Fracture, Travel, and End.

Nine was created with ink on bristol board.

#1 Deadbeat Parent Award

Fall 2025
Loss Wax Casting, Cast Aluminum

#1 Deadbeat Parent Award is a small sculpture cast in aluminum to commemorate all the deadbeats in the world. The central skull plays into the term deadbeat, accentuating the idea that a deadbeat being alive or not would not make a noticeable difference.

#1 Deadbeat Parent Award was created with lost wax casting and cast aluminum.

Dream of Pink Lemonade
Dream of Summer

Spring 2026
Silk Screen Printing - Photoshop

Dream of Pink Lemonade / Dream of Summer are abstracted versions of portraits with varied colors. A silhouette of a person’s face with an abstract halftone design inside creates a rather simplistic version of a portrait. Heavy linework above and behind the head pulls the viewer’s eyes horizontally across the design. The background is a halftone gradient printed in a color gradient to create a unique visual with the paper showing through.

Dream of Pink Lemonade / Dream of Summer are silkscreen prints that had their stencils created in Adobe Photoshop.

Marble / Marble Misprint

Spring 2026
Silk Screen Printing - Photoshop - Blender

Marble / Marble Misprint is an abstract print that wildly draws the viewer’s attention across the page. The background is a halftone abstract design made to look similar to marble but far smoother. The subject of the piece is not meant to be representational but rather appear differently to different viewers.

Marble / Marble Misprint are silkscreen prints that had their stencils created in Adobe Photoshop and Blender.
