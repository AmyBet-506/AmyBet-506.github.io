Rose Overture

Summer 2024
Blender - Adobe Substance Painter

Rose Overture is an exercise in ceremonial weapon design. The visual language of this piece highlights what an ornate ceremonial weapon may look like within a Sci-Fi environment. The rifle is not entirely pristine and has noticeable edge wear and some minor scratches to demonstrate that this weapon has been in use for a time but lacks the level of weathering that would be seen on a battle worn rifle.

Rose Overture was modeled, sculpted, and rendered in Blender. The textures for this piece were created in Adobe Substance Painter and Photoshop.

Druid Helmet

Spring 2024
Zbrush - Maya - Adobe Substance Painter - Blender

Druids are often portrayed as sages and priests that revered the natural world, Druid Helmet leans into these common portrayals by depicting a mask made from a deer skull. The inspiration behind this piece partially comes from plague doctor masks, a large oversized mask that significantly changes the silhouette of the face and looks like, or in the case of Druid Helmet, is a skull. The choice to have this mask be a deer’s skull rather than a bird’s was because deer skulls have a very odd shape and the antlers additionally change the face’s silhouette.

Druid Helmet was sculpted in ZBrush, retopologized in Maya, textured in Adobe Substance Painter, and rendered in Blender.

The Duelist

Fall 2023
Maya - Adobe Substance Painter

The Duelist is a battle hardened character who has just enough armor to get by without sacrificing her mobility. Scratches and imperfections cover her metal armor from previous battles along with her bandaged off hand. Asymmetrical balance is achieved with her design by having her wear a glove on one hand and bandages on the other.

The Duelist was modeled and rendered in Maya with her hair being created using XGen. The textures for this piece were created in Adobe Substance Painter.

Mushling

Spring 2024
ZBrush

Mushling is a strange subterranean creature sculpted to look like an amalgamation of several living beings. The overall form of the creature draws inspiration from gorillas while the face draws inspiration from both spiders and moths. The false eyes above the creature’s true head pair with its hulking figure and large frontal claws in order to give it an intimidating appearance. While this creature is meant to look fearsome it is a largely docile herbivore.

Mushling was sculpted and painted in ZBrush.

The Cleric

Spring 2026
Blender - Adobe Substance Painter

By evoking imagery similar to statues of Justice there is an air of familiarity about The Cleric. Her spikey silhouette and dark color palette create an imposing visual language rather than one of fairness. Within this piece blind justice is subverted to become blind faith, with the hands of her god directly blinding her as she carries out her own version of justice. The golden spikes on the helmet create visuals reminiscent of a nimbus from traditional western religious art which informs viewers of The Cleric’s holiness at a glance. Her long flowing skirt was simulated to give it a bit of a weighty look.

The Cleric was modeled, sculpted, and rendered entirely in Blender. The textures for her were created in Adobe Substance Painter.
