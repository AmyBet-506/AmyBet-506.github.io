Anonymous Woman

Fall 2022
Photoshop

Anonymous Woman is a piece focusing on color theory and visual composition. The visual language of this piece invokes both outer space and the flowing of a river. The piece’s composition draws the viewer’s eye diagonally across the piece in part due to the direction of the background but also due to the direction the woman is facing. The smoke behind the woman invokes angelic imagery while helping to balance the composition and pull the eye from the background down to her pixelated face.

Anonymous Woman was created in Adobe Photoshop.

Frothing Orange Liquid Advertisment

Fall 2022
Photoshop - After Effects - Premiere Pro

Frothing Orange Liquid is a satirical video piece made to critique modern food and diet culture. This work mimics the upbeat and bright nature of modern advertisements while explicitly advertising the unhealthy effects of the product.

Frothing Orange Liquid was made in Adobe Photoshop, After Effects, and Premiere Pro.

Urchins

Fall 2023
Photoshop

Urchins is a digital drawing done in photoshop of a barren undersea environment, highlighting the devastation caused by purple sea urchin populations in coastal kelp. The piece is very monotone in its colors, largely being blue and purple while a few plants stand out as green. This piece was done before I had learned how to properly use many digital tools but the environmental messaging of the piece remains important.

Urchins was created in Adobe Photoshop.

Vaporwave Gallery

Fall 2022
Photoshop

Vaporwave Gallery is a stylistic study in the vaporwave aesthetic. The three pieces all utilize similar colors to give them a warm and vibrant feel without becoming overly loud. Similarly each piece has a very vertical composition, each one forces the eye to be drawn towards the central subject of each piece.

All pieces in Vaporwave Gallery were created in Adobe Photoshop.
Sunken_Bells
Spring 2025
Sunken Bells is an audio piece that invokes the both serene and ominous sounds of water to grab the listener’s attention. The audio sources for this piece were heavily edited to fully fit the intended audio aesthetic. Ice cubes spinning in a glass, a dishwasher, trains moving, and metal doors groaning were just some of the sources for the audio.

Sunken Bells was created using Adobe Premiere Pro.
