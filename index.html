About Me

Hey, chat! I’m Amelia and this is my very modern portfolio. In case the blinkies didn’t make it clear, I’m trans, I don’t use AI, and I enjoy the internet aesthetics of the late 90s and early 2000s (or at least the modern interpretation of the aesthetic). Besides that, I also enjoy coffee, listening to music, brutalist architecture, playing video games, and obviously, making art. I started playing around with Blender during the summer of 2020, right after everything started shutting down. I started with simple tutorials, gradually adding my own flair to them until I had enough knowledge of the program to fully make my own art. Eventually this coalesced in the following year when I decided that STEM didn’t really work with my brain and that I wanted to go to college for art; that, of course, landed me quite a few concerned looks from my family. After high school, I ended up at a community college for two years where I took a few art classes specifically focused on digital 3D art. I transferred to San Jose State University in the fall of 2024, where I took a wide variety of art classes, from metal casting to screen printing and everything in between. Having to learn such a wide variety of mediums meant that I had to work on my theory in order to produce high quality art before my technical skill was able to stand on its own. While this was a daunting task, it forced me to figure out how to pick up new mediums quickly and transfer my artistic knowledge between them. Picking up new methods of creating art had quickly become second nature to me and now I am confident in my ability to work in any medium even if I haven’t ever touched it before. While I hold a large bias towards 3D art I love 2D mediums across the board. Recently I have been picking up screen printing and find it fascinating how similar it is to digitally drawing with masks.
